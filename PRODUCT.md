# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Worldwide visitors discovering the Sector 6 Mosque in Bhilai, including local community members, travelers, students, and people interested in architecture or civic life. Visitors may arrive with any faith or no religious background, so the story should be welcoming, clear, and culturally respectful without assuming prior knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Present the mosque as a living place built from architecture, worship, service, and community. This project focuses on the cinematic storytelling section of a larger mosque website. The wider site will later contain prayer times, mosque services, charity, zakat, community programs, and practical visitor information. Success means visitors understand the building's main architectural parts, feel the calm identity of the place, and remain curious about the community around it.

## Brand Personality

Sacred, cinematic, calm. The voice is secular and globally accessible: reverent toward the mosque and Islamic visual culture, factual about architectural functions, and welcoming to every visitor.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic luxury templates, commercial real-estate presentations, tourism advertising, neon spectacle, gamified interfaces, ornamental excess, glass-heavy card layouts, and religious claims that cannot be verified from the available references. Motion should feel composed and architectural rather than flashy.

## Design Principles

1. Let the mosque remain the main subject in every scene.
2. Teach through camera movement and spatial focus before adding explanatory text.
3. Build emotional intensity gradually, from atmosphere to form to human purpose.
4. Keep language understandable to visitors without architectural or religious expertise.
5. Design every story beat as a replaceable component so the larger mosque website can evolve around it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for text, focus states, contrast, and keyboard use. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with a calm sequence of static camera views and gentle fades. Keep the Arabic particle moment decorative, while the English mosque name and architectural descriptions remain available as real text. The story must remain understandable when WebGL is unavailable.
